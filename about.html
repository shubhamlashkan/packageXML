ABOUT PACKAGEXML.DEV

Practical tools for Salesforce work

PackageXML.dev brings a few useful Salesforce tools together in one place. They are built to make common development and administration tasks a little quicker, whether you are preparing metadata, reviewing a formula, or checking access changes.

Why we built it

Some Salesforce tasks are small but take more time than they should. Building a metadata manifest, making a long formula readable, or checking what changed between two permission files can involve a lot of manual work. PackageXML.dev focuses on those everyday jobs and provides straightforward tools to help with them.
