Salesforce Formula Formatter FAQs

Answers about formatting Salesforce formulas and validation rules.

How do I format a Salesforce formula online?

Paste your Salesforce formula into the input editor. The formatter organizes the expression with readable indentation. Review the output and use Copy output to copy the formatted formula.

Can I format Salesforce validation rules with this tool?

Yes. You can use the formatter to improve the readability of validation rule formulas, including conditions built with functions such as AND(), OR(), NOT(), IF(), and ISCHANGED().

Does this Salesforce formula formatter support nested IF statements?

Yes. It can organize nested IF() expressions so that each level of the formula is easier to inspect and maintain.

Can I format formulas containing CASE(), AND(), and OR()?

Yes. The formatter is intended for Salesforce formula expressions that use functions such as CASE(), AND(), OR(), and other nested logic.

Does formatting validate Salesforce formula syntax?

No. Formatting improves indentation and readability, but it does not guarantee that a formula is syntactically valid or will compile. Verify the formula in Salesforce.

Is my Salesforce formula uploaded to a server?

The formatter runs in your browser and does not require a Salesforce login. Avoid entering confidential information into any online tool unless you have reviewed its implementation and your organization's policies.

Is this Salesforce formula formatter free?

Yes. The formatter is free to use and does not require registration, installation, or a Salesforce login.
